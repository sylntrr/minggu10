About me

I'm a third-semester Information Systems student at Universitas Dian Nuswantoro in Semarang. I'm currently deepening my skills in UI/UX Design because I'm passionate about creating digital experiences that are simple, effective, and centered on users.

Through various study projects and design explorations, I continue to grow my abilities in user research, wireframing, and prototyping with Figma. I enjoy applying design thinking to turn real user needs into thoughtful, relevant solutions, such as the EVO badminton court booking system and a task reminder feature for Dinusverse.

Before university, I studied Computer and Network Engineering at vocational high school, then did an administration internship at Telkom Semarang. That background helps me understand the technical side of a system, not just how it looks. I'm now also part of the Multimedia division at Dian Nuswantoro Computer Club, where I learn to collaborate in a team and create visual content.
